About

Planned on paper.
Delivered on site.

I'm Ofogba Onoriode Festus, a Civil Engineer and Project Coordinator with hands-on experience across residential and small commercial building projects. I've supervised daily site activities, coordinated craftsmen and subcontractors, managed procurement, and kept projects on schedule without compromising quality.

I translate drawings and specifications into actionable work plans, resolve on-site issues, and keep stakeholders aligned from start to finish. HSE certification backs that work with safe methods, hazard awareness, and compliance as part of how I run every site.

I also bring a front-end web development background, diploma-trained in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which helps me present projects clearly, build client-facing sites, and deliver work that looks as professional as the structures behind it.
