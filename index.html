About

Planned on paper.
Delivered on site.

I’m Ofogba Onoriode Festus, a civil engineer and project coordinator with hands-on experience across residential and small commercial building projects. I have supervised daily site activities, coordinated craftsmen and subcontractors, managed procurement, and kept projects on schedule without compromising quality.

I turn drawings and specifications into workable programmes, resolve issues on site, and keep stakeholders aligned from start to finish. HSE certification sits inside that work: safe methods, hazard awareness, and compliance on every site I run.

I also hold a diploma in front-end web development (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript). That helps me present projects clearly, build client-facing sites, and make the digital work look as considered as the structures behind it.
